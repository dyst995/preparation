## 1. Core phrases (memorize)

| # | Phrase | When to use | Example |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **It depends on...** | Soften a yes/no; buy thinking time | It depends on the situation. |
| 2 | **As far as I know...** | You’re not 100% sure | As far as I know, the meeting is at 3. |
| 3 | **To be honest...** | Soften an opinion | To be honest, I’m not convinced. |
| 4 | **What I mean is...** | Restart / clarify after a messy sentence | What I mean is, we should wait. |
| 5 | **Let me rephrase that.** | You said it badly; reset cleanly | Let me rephrase that. The deadline is Friday. |
| 6 | **That makes sense.** | Show you understood | That makes sense. Thanks for explaining. |
| 7 | **Could you clarify...?** | You didn’t fully understand | Could you clarify what you need from me? |
| 8 | **I see what you mean.** | Agreement / empathy | I see what you mean. |
| 9 | **In other words...** | Simplify your own idea | In other words, we need more time. |
| 10 | **The point is...** | Cut to the main idea | The point is, we can’t ship today. |

**Say aloud (fill blanks):**

## 2. Vocabulary for today (10 words)

| Word | Meaning (simple) | Useful sentence |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **roughly** | approximately | It takes roughly two hours. |
| **slightly** | a little | I’m slightly late. |
| **eventually** | in the end / after some time | We eventually found a solution. |
| **currently** | right now | I’m currently working on that. |
| **recently** | not long ago | I recently finished the course. |
| **actually** | in fact (careful: not “currently”) | Actually, I already sent it. |
| **basically** | in simple terms | Basically, we need approval first. |
| **specifically** | exactly / in particular | Specifically, I need the PDF. |
| **reasonable** | fair / makes sense | That’s a reasonable request. |
| **reliable** | you can trust it | He’s very reliable. |

**Don’t confuse:**
- **actually** = in fact  
- **currently** = right now  
Georgian speakers often mix these.

## 3. Natural upgrades (replace weak English)

| Instead of... | Say... |
| --- | --- |
| I think maybe we can... | **I’d suggest** we... |
| For me it’s hard | **I’m struggling with**... |
| I want to say that... | **What I mean is...** / **The point is...** |
| I don’t know how to explain | **Let me rephrase that.** |
| Yes yes I understand | **That makes sense.** / **I see what you mean.** |
| More better | **Better** / **Much better** |
| I am agree | **I agree** |
| Explain me | **Could you explain that to me?** |

## 4. Mini dialogues (read out loud)

**A — Soft disagreement**  
- I don’t think that’s the best option.  
- **To be honest**, I’d choose the simpler approach.  
- **The point is**, we don’t have enough time.

**B — Clarifying**  
- Sorry, **could you clarify** what you need by Friday?  
- **What I mean is**, do you need the full report or just a summary?

**C — Buying time without freezing**  
- **It depends on** the requirements.  
- **As far as I know**, we haven’t decided yet.  
- **Let me rephrase that** — we need one more day.
