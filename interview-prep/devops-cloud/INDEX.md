Why this track matters for you

Your CV explicitly lists Docker, AWS (S3, SNS), Nginx, and Fastlane under DevOps & Cloud, and you have real, specific, verifiable stories:
- **Travel2Georgia**: you designed and developed the complete platform (customer website, admin dashboard, backend) and managed the full lifecycle "from architecture and database design to deployment and production infrastructure," explicitly configuring "cloud infrastructure, Docker, Nginx, SSL, and domain management."
- **Orient Logic / Online School**: you "built Android and iOS CI/CD pipelines with GitLab Runner and Fastlane, automating build and signing processes for release candidates," and used this to reduce release friction while also driving down crash rates in production.

This track is not about becoming a full-time DevOps engineer - it's about being able to speak fluently and specifically about deployment, infrastructure, and CI/CD as a full-stack/mobile engineer who has actually shipped and operated production systems end to end, which is a real differentiator over candidates who "just write code and hand it off."

Suggested study order (any day)

1. **01 Docker** - foundational; almost every follow-up assumes you know containers.
2. **02 Nginx/SSL/VPS** - your strongest, most concrete story (Travel2Georgia); learn the theory that backs up what you already did.
3. **04 Git/CI basics** - your other strongest story (GitLab Runner + Fastlane); also very commonly asked about generically.
4. **03 AWS S3/SNS** - narrower scope, but a clean, well-defined topic that's easy to nail if prepared.
5. **05 Interview Questions** - rehearse the deployment story drills last, once the theory chapters are fresh.

Daily drill (any day)

1. Pick one chapter.
2. Read the topics and check off what you can already teach out loud.
3. Answer 5 interview questions with no notes.
4. Rehearse the Travel2Georgia deployment story (chapter 05) out loud, timing yourself to 60-90 seconds.
5. Write down weak spots for the next pass.
