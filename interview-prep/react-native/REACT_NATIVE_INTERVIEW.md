## Suggested study order (8–10h / day)

Given your CV, prioritize in this order:

1. **01 Fundamentals** + **07 Native Modules** — your differentiator
2. **06 Performance** + **12 Upgrades & Stability** — crash-rate stories need this depth
3. **03 State** + **04 Navigation** + **05 Networking** — daily RN interview core
4. **08 Push/Firebase** + **09 Fintech UX** — EasyPay / Wizer / Clean House
5. **11 CI/CD** + **13 Security** — senior/fintech expectations
6. **02 Architecture** + **10 Testing** — design and quality bar
7. **14 Behavioral** — rehearse every day for 30–45 minutes

---

## Daily drill (any day)

1. Pick one chapter
2. Read topics + check off what you can already teach
3. Answer 5 interview questions out loud (no notes)
4. Rehearse one STAR story from chapter 14
5. Write down weak spots for next pass
